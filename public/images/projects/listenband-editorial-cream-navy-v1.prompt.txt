Use case: stylized-concept / editorial product still life.
Create one finished landscape project cover image for ListenBand, intended for a sophisticated personal portfolio called The Personal Journal. Landscape 3:2 composition, high resolution.
Project grounding: ListenBand is a desktop Obsidian plugin for language learning with video listening, timestamped English-Chinese transcripts, offline dictionary and on-demand translation. This image is conceptual project artwork.
Art direction: sophisticated independent culture and design magazine, striking restrained graphic editorial composition, tactile cream uncoated paper and deep navy printing. Warm cream #f3efdf occupies about 75% of the image, dark ink navy #173e55 25%. No coastal scenery, no sky, no sea, no palm, no bright blue, no gradients, no tech startup aesthetic.
Composition: overhead editorial still-life on warm cream paper, with deliberately asymmetric grid. Upper left a very large beautiful high-contrast Bodoni-style serif wordmark reading exactly "ListenBand", deep navy, finely drawn elegant letters, perfectly legible. Just below in small widely spaced sans serif: "LISTEN / LEARN / KEEP". Fine navy horizontal rule adds quiet magazine structure.
Across lower central and right portions, a beautifully photographed sculptural pair of matte midnight-navy wired over-ear headphones, physically credible and refined, rests partly on an open ivory listening notebook. Notebook has sparse navy timestamped transcript typography, a short English phrase "Listen closely." and beneath it a short Chinese translation "仔细聆听。" with clear fine typography, one subtle pale blue-gray underlined segment, plenty of blank paper. An elegantly curling cream paper ribbon with sparse timestamp marks emerges from the notebook and curls towards the left, forming a restrained waveform-like rhythm. Headphone cable elegantly echoes the curve. No separate audio device, no laptop or phone, no fake software interface.
Visual balance: generous negative space at left below headline, still-life objects have strong graphic silhouettes and palpable paper and fabric texture, soft directional daylight with delicate long shadows, subtle analog film grain, premium magazine art photography mixed with exceptionally controlled graphic design. Sophisticated, intellectual, calm and collectible. Avoid clutter, rounded cards, UI widgets, purple Obsidian logos, promotional badges, watermarks, random tiny text. Restrict text to title, subtitle and sparse notebook transcript/timecodes. Keep title and subjects within 8 percent safe margins; artwork fills the canvas, no external frame or surrounding mockup.
